Passage studies — the sixteen-tool digs

A full exegetical report on each unit, in canonical order. ★ marks a theologically load-bearing (HIGH-weight) passage.

1:1–11

Partners in the Gospel

The letter's overture — greeting, thanksgiving, and prayer that plant almost every theme the letter develops.

 1:12–26

To Live is Christ

Imprisonment as the gospel's advance; the righteous sufferer's vindication (Job 13:16); "to live is Christ, to die is gain."

 1:27–30

Worthy of the Gospel

The letter's thesis: stand firm as one, unafraid, under opposition — with suffering reframed as granted grace.

 2:1–11 ★

The Mind of Christ

The Christ-hymn — self-emptying then exalted; the highest Christology (Isa 45:23) deployed for the humblest ethic.

 2:12–18

Work Out Your Salvation

Effort grounded in God's working; the Deuteronomy 32:5 reversal; the church as luminaries in a dark world.

 2:19–30

Two Who Spent Themselves

Timothy and Epaphroditus — the hymn enacted in two named lives; the mind of Christ made imitable.

 3:1–11 ★

Everything as Loss

No confidence in the flesh; Paul enacts the hymn ("count all loss"); righteousness received as the doorway to knowing Christ.

 3:10–21 ★

Pressing On; Citizens of Heaven

The hymn stamped on the disciple; grace grounds the straining; heavenly citizenship and the transforming Saviour (Ps 8).

 4:1–9

Stand Firm: Unity, Joy, Peace

The thesis cashed out on Euodia and Syntyche; the peace of God garrisoning heart and mind; do not be anxious.

 4:10–23

The Secret of Contentment

The letter's close: the gift received as worship; Christ-sufficient contentment; the gospel in Caesar's household.
