## Texts for the series

- **Substrate:** the Hebrew (WLC for observation; BHS from Logos for anything turning on the apparatus), with the Greek alongside.
- **Study text:** NASB95.
- **Pulpit text:** ESV, declared 28 September 2026. Every dig compares the ESV and NIV84 against the NASB95, and says where the ESV changes what the congregation will hear.

## How each sermon unit is built

1. **Solo digs** on the dig units inside the sermon unit. These are the seventeen units from the overview, the exegetical grain. HIGH units always get one. Standard units get one only where marked "Recommended".
2. **Unit dig (consolidation)** when a sermon unit spans more than one dig unit. It builds on the solo digs and adds the unit-level layer. It becomes the report used for the sermon. Where a sermon unit is a single dig unit (Sermons 2, 6, 7, 9), the solo dig *is* the unit report.
3. **Sermon backbone** (point-purpose), from the unit report. This needs the pulpit text.
4. **Sermon review** and final manuscript (.odt), when a draft exists.

Superseded reports are kept in the Job folder and marked at the top, never deleted.

## The introductory sermon

**Decided 28 September 2026:** the series opens with a sermon on the whole book, built on 1:9 ("for nothing?") and 42:7–8 (God's verdict on Job and the friends). It gives the congregation the book's shape before the long speech-cycles, so that Sermon 1 can go straight into the prologue. Its backbone is drawn from the book overview, the sweep and the claim audit, with the anchor texts re-verified. **Backbone ✓ done (28 Sep):** `point-purpose-job-book`. **Series question (settled 28 Sep), asked two ways:** "Is God worth it — even if he gives you nothing?" and "Do you love God — or only what he gives you?"
