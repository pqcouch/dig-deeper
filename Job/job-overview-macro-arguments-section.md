## Cross-Pericope Macro-Arguments

*What the eighteen completed dig-deeper runs jointly demonstrate, as distinct from what any one passage shows. Full working in `job-dig-deeper-macro-synthesis.md`. Each argument below recurs across at least three pericopes or is authorised by the New Testament across at least two; confidence per the house key — NT-authorised **high**, converging text-first **strong**, looser trajectory **moderate**.*

**1. The book is made out of its own words.** `[T]` **strong** — eight of the eighteen runs report this independently. The speakers quote one another, themselves and God; Job's densest verbal relationships are internal. **Quote every line of the dialogue with its speaker and ask what the line does in that mouth** — a sentence true in Eliphaz's is an accusation in Bildad's and a protest in Job's.

**2. The end answers the beginning thematically — and the lexical design failed four chance baselines.** Chains **high**; design **moderate**. Four runs claimed a late block was lexically welded to an early chapter (speech 1 ← ch. 3; speech 2 ← ch. 29; epilogue ← 13:8–10; epilogue ← ch. 8). **Claim audit 6 broke all four**: 85–100 % of same-length Job blocks score as high against their own best partner, and in two cases the real partner was elsewhere. The individual chains stand, two of them stronger than claimed — **סָכַךְ "a" in exactly two verses of the Hebrew Bible (3:23, 38:8)**; **רֵאשִׁית with אַחֲרִית in exactly two verses of Job (8:7, 42:12)**. **Preach the correspondence; drop the proof.**

**3. Both sides quarry the same Scriptures — the instances, not the pattern.** `[T]` **instances strong; the pattern low** `[S: audit 7]` — three demonstrated instances: **Ps 8:5 at 7:17, 15:14 and 25:5–6**; **Deut 32:39 at 5:18 and 10:7**; **Ps 107:40–42 split across 12:21, 12:24 and 5:11, 15–16**. **Audit 7 broke the pattern and kept the best instance.** Of 31,134 candidate rare lemma-pairs, **six show the same shape and none is one of the claimed three**, so "both sides contest the same texts" describes what any book-length disputation over a shared Scripture must look like. **The Deut 32:39 instance is withdrawn — Eliphaz takes its wound/heal half and Job its none-delivers half, so it is not an inversion.** **שֹׁפֵךְ בּוּז עַל־נְדִיבִים at Ps 107:40 and Job 12:21 is exactly two canon verses and stands at high.** The book still stages a contest over *how* Scripture applies to this man — which is why "the friends said true things" is neither a defence nor a difficulty — but **preach that as a reading of the dialogue, not as a measured design.**

**4. The book tracks the direction of speech, and its verdict falls on it.** `[T]` **strong** for the distribution (§6, §7, §12, §18); **moderate and the project's own** for the resolution. Speech *about* God to others, *to* God, and *for* God are distinguished across four runs, and **the clause לֹא דִבַּרְתֶּם אֵלַי נְכוֹנָה stands in 42:7 and 42:8 and nowhere else in the Hebrew Bible** — with אֵלַי, "to me". **The Logos round of 16 Nov found no commentator in the library resting on the preposition**; present it as this project's reading.

**5. "Answer" is demanded, denied, and granted.** `[T]` **strong** — 5:1 ("is there anyone who will answer you?") → 13:22, 14:15 → the storm, where **ענה appears five times in the frame and never inside the creation tour.** God grants the hearing and withholds the explanation, and the book treats that as sufficient.

**6. The mediator Job cannot find is the book's longest thread.** **strong** — **מוֹכִיחַ** 9:33 → **עֵד** 16:19 → **ערב** 17:3 → **גֹּאֵל** 19:25 → Elihu's mediator and ransom 33:23–24 → **פָּלַל in exactly two verses of the book, 42:8 and 42:10, both intercession for someone else.** At the end there is still no umpire: God simply pronounces, and the man who wanted a mediator becomes one for his accusers. **The gain is in the gap, not in closing it early.**

**7. Comfort is the book's test-case, and the only comforting that works is wordless.** `[T]` **strong, raised by audit 7** `[S: audit 7]` — **נָחַם in exactly seven verses: 2:11, 7:13, 16:2, 21:34, 29:25, 42:6, 42:11**, with **נוּד + נָחַם in Job only at 2:11 and 42:11**. **And audit 7 supplied the lemma the runs had missed: נוד + נָחַם + רָעָה + בּוֹא עָלָיו is unique in the whole Hebrew Bible to Job 2:11 and 42:11, with the agent of the evil withheld at the first (qal הַבָּאָה) and named at the second (hiphil הֵבִיא יְהוָה).** The only design claim of sixteen that audit 7 confirmed. Three men travelled from three countries with a theology and failed; relatives arrived late with bread and succeeded, explaining nothing.

**8. The New Testament quarries the whole cast of Job, not only Job.** **high** — **1 Cor 3:19 cites Eliphaz (5:13) with γέγραπται**; **Rom 11:33's ἀνεξιχνίαστος occurs in the Greek OT only at 5:9, 9:10 and 34:24** — Eliphaz, Job, Elihu; **Rom 11:35 renders 41:11, God's own words**; **Phil 1:19 reproduces Swete's 13:16 exactly**; **Jas 5:11 gives Job ὑπομονή and the prophets μακροθυμία in consecutive verses.** The NT's willingness to cite Eliphaz as Scripture is the strongest corrective to a flat "the friends were wrong" reading.

**9. ~~Chapter 28 as the book's hinge.~~ DISCARDED — do not insert this item.** `[S: audit 7]` Audit 7 tested it hardest, by an auditor who did not know the project had proposed it, and it failed every test: **design null 98–99 % at all four ceilings**; chapters 31, 9 and 3 all beat chapter 28 as the divine speeches' partner; 23–40 % of arbitrary windows match or beat the speeches as chapter 28's partner; the chain shape replicates for five other chapters; **the epilogue is chapter 28's *worst* partner at Dice 0.0000**; and **42:5 stands in Job's closing reply, not the epilogue — a destination I misidentified.** **What to carry instead, and it is better: שַׁחַץ stands in exactly two verses of the Hebrew Bible, Job 28:8 and 41:26 [Eng 41:34], in the same bound phrase בְּנֵי־שָׁחַץ — the proud beasts have not trodden the miner's path; Leviathan is king over them. One canon-exclusive contact, worth more than the hinge.**

**What this adds up to.** **Job is a book about speech.** Its characters argue by requoting one another; they contest the same Scriptures pulled opposite ways; the text marks who speaks to whom and makes its final verdict turn on it; and God assesses not what anyone suffered but what they said. **The friends' error is not a false theology but a true one used as a diagnosis.** Around that runs a longing the book raises and does not satisfy — and the preaching gain is in the gap.

**A method note for the remaining sermons.** Across claim audits 3–6 the sweep established which evidence holds here and which does not: **lemma-verified chains hold** (ten of seventeen distributions in audit 6 were exactly right, and everything that broke, broke on grammar — a stem, a preposition, a voice); **designs built on those chains repeatedly do not** (four discarded in audit 6 alone); **window ranks are not evidence without a null, and must hold at two parameter settings before being quoted.** A congregation can be told that two words stand together in only two verses of the Bible. They should not be told that a statistical shape proves authorial intent.
