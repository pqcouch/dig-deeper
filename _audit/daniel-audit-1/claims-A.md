# Claims file — Auditor A (single contacts)

Each claim is stated as the proposing reports state it, counts included. Daniel text: WLC (Hebrew and Aramaic); Greek: Rahlfs Old Greek and Theodotion exports; NT: SBLGNT. References are English numbering; Daniel 3:31–4:34 Hebrew = 4:1–37 English, Hebrew 6:1 = English 5:31, Hebrew 6:2–29 = English 6:1–28.

## A1 — Isa 26:19 behind Dan 12:2
Stated: קִיץ ("to awake") + עָפָר ("dust") in one verse — **2 verses in the Hebrew Bible: Isa 26:19 and Dan 12:2**. Suggested **high**. Test: the count, positive control, rivals (Job 14:12; Ps 22:30; Isa 66:24; other awakening texts with שָׁכַב/יָשֵׁן); verdict.

## A2 — Isa 66:24 behind Dan 12:2
Stated: דֵּרָאוֹן ("abhorrence, contempt") — **the only two occurrences in the Hebrew Bible, Isa 66:24 and Dan 12:2**. Suggested **high**. Test: count; whether the two senses match (corpses seen vs those who awake); rival explanations (a rare word in a shared idiom pool); verdict. Also test whether A1 and A2 together (Isaiah's first and last resurrection/judgement texts) strengthen each other or are independent.

## A3 — Isa 52:13; 53:11 behind Dan 11:33, 35; 12:3, 10
Stated: צָדַק hiphil ("to make righteous") + רַבִּים ("the many") — **2 verses in the Hebrew Bible, Isa 53:11 and Dan 12:3**; שָׂכַל ("to have insight, prosper") opens the Servant song (Isa 52:13), and Daniel's מַשְׂכִּילִים ("those who have insight") are the subjects at 11:33, 35; 12:3, 10; שָׂכַל with צָדַק in one verse: Dan 12:3 only. Suggested **high**. Test: counts (note: does the lemma search distinguish hiphil? check the actual forms); positive control; rivals; verdict.

## A4 — Isa 10:23, 25 behind Dan 9:27 and 11:36
Stated: כָּלָה ("complete destruction") + חָרַץ ("to decree") — **3 verses: Isa 10:23, Isa 28:22, Dan 9:27**; כָּלָה ("to be finished") + זַעַם ("indignation") — **3 verses: Isa 10:25, Ezek 22:31, Dan 11:36**; Dan 11:36 holds both. Rival named: Isa 28:22. Suggested **high**. Test: the counts (two different lemmas spelled כלה — check which is which); whether 11:36 has the noun or the verb; rival weighting between Isa 10 and Isa 28; verdict.

## A5 — Num 24:24 (and 24:14) behind Dan 11:30 (and 10:14)
Stated: צִי ("ship") + כִּתִּים ("Kittim") — **2 verses: Num 24:24 and Dan 11:30**; "in the latter days" (בְּאַחֲרִית הַיָּמִים) at Num 24:14 and Dan 10:14, but that phrase stands in 13 verses (idiom). The Old Greek renders Kittim at 11:30 as Ῥωμαῖοι ("Romans"); Theodotion Κίτιοι. Suggested: **high (Kittim)**; Numbers 24 a **live source (provisional)**. Test: counts, Greek readings (Rahlfs exports), whether a "live source" survives on one exclusive contact plus one idiom; verdict.

## A6 — Hab 2:3 behind Dan 8:17, 19; 10:14; 11:27, 35
Stated: חָזוֹן ("vision") + קֵץ ("end") in one verse — **2 verses: Hab 2:3 and Dan 8:17**; the ordered phrase עוֹד חָזוֹן ("yet a vision") — **2 verses: Hab 2:3 and Dan 10:14**; Dan 11:27, 35 add "the end is still for the appointed time" (קֵץ + מוֹעֵד). Suggested **high, live**. Test: counts, positive controls, whether קֵץ + מוֹעֵד is itself rare (Hab 2:3 has both), rivals (Ezek 7; 12:21–28 "the days are near and the fulfilment of every vision"); verdict.

## A7 — Ezek 1; 9–10 behind Dan 10:5–6; 12:6–7 (and 7:9–10, cross-language)
Stated: נְחֹשֶׁת קָלָל ("burnished bronze") — קָלָל occurs **only at Ezek 1:7 and Dan 10:6**; לָבֻשׁ ("clothed") + בַּדִּים ("linen") in **12 verses** (Lev 6:3; 16:4, 23, 32 — the high priest; Ezek 9:2, 3; 10:2, 6, 7; Dan 10:5; 12:6, 7); rival: Lev 16; לַפִּיד ("torch") + אֵשׁ ("fire") in 6 verses incl. Ezek 1:13 and Dan 10:6; also the throne-vision of 7:9–10 (Aramaic) with thrones, fire and wheels against Ezek 1 and 10. Suggested **high, live**. Test: counts; rival (Lev 16 priest vs Ezek 9–10 scribe-figure); further contacts in Dan 10:6 (תַּרְשִׁישׁ "beryl"; "the sound of his words like the sound of a multitude"); verdict.

## A8 — Deut 32:40 behind Dan 12:7
Stated: שָׁמַיִם ("heaven") + חַי ("living") + עוֹלָם ("ever") in one verse — **2 verses: Deut 32:40 and Dan 12:7**; swearing + living + ever: Dan 12:7 only. Suggested **high**. Test: counts; the oath formula's spread (חַי־אָנִי, "as I live", elsewhere; raising the hand to swear — Gen 14:22; Exod 6:8; Ezek 20); verdict.

## A9 — Genesis 40–41 behind Daniel 1–2 (and 5:29), with Exodus 7:11
Stated: (a) פָּעַם ("to be troubled") + רוּחַ ("spirit") — **4 verses: Gen 41:8, Judg 13:25, Dan 2:1, 2:3**; (b) חַרְטֹם ("magician") + חֲלוֹם ("dream") in one verse — **Gen 41:8 and Dan 2:2 only**; (c) זֹעֲפִים ("dejected", of faces) — Gen 40:6 and Dan 1:10 (the lemma's other two occurrences, Prov 19:3 and 2 Chr 26:19, are verbal); (d) cross-language: "not in me" (Gen 41:16 / Dan 2:30); "God has made known" (Gen 41:25, 28, 39 / Dan 2:28–29, 45); "found a man" (Gen 41:38 / Dan 2:25); promotion and robe/gold chain (Gen 41:41–42 / Dan 2:48; 5:29); (e) חַרְטֹם + מְכַשֵּׁף ("sorcerer") — **2 verses: Exod 7:11 and Dan 2:2**. Suggested **high (Joseph); moderate–high (Exodus 7)**. Test: each count, positive controls, rivals; whether Daniel's court-tale features are generic to court tales (Esther; Ahiqar `[S]`) rather than drawn from Genesis; verdict for Joseph and for Exodus 7 separately.

## A10 — Deut 7:9 and Neh 1:5 with Dan 9:4
Stated: the ordered phrase שֹׁמֵר הַבְּרִית וְהַחֶסֶד לְאֹהֲבָיו ("who keeps the covenant and the lovingkindness to those who love him") — **2 verses: Deut 7:9, Dan 9:4**; Neh 1:5 has the longer sequence הָאֵל הַגָּדוֹל וְהַנּוֹרָא שֹׁמֵר הַבְּרִית with וָחֶסֶד (no article); Neh 9:32 adds הַגִּבּוֹר. Claimed: Deut 7:9 is the source; Neh 1:5 is the nearest *neighbour* (a canonical handoff). Suggested **high**. Test: full comparison of Dan 9:4, Neh 1:5, Neh 9:32, Deut 7:9, 7:21, 10:17, 1 Kgs 8:23, 2 Chr 6:14; which is closest to Dan 9:4 word for word; whether "formula of the penitential prayer tradition" explains all; verdict on (a) Deut 7:9 as source and (b) Neh 1:5 as closest.

## A11 — 2 Chr 36:7 behind Dan 1:2
Stated: כְּלִי ("vessel") + Nebuchadnezzar + הֵיכָל ("temple, palace") — **1 verse: 2 Chr 36:7**; "vessels" + Nebuchadnezzar + "house": 4 verses (Jer 28:3; Ezra 1:7; 2 Chr 36:7, 10); Dan 1:2 has "the vessels of the house of God" brought to "the house of his god". Suggested **high**. Test: counts (note Dan 1:2 itself lacks הֵיכָל — is the "1 verse" count a fair test?); rivals (2 Kgs 24:13; Jer 27:16–22; Ezra 1:7); direction of dependence (Chronicles may postdate Daniel's sources); verdict.

## A12 — Revelation 1:1; 4:1; 22:6 framed by Theodotion's Daniel 2:28–29, 45
Stated: ἃ δεῖ γενέσθαι ("what must take place") in Theodotion Dan 2:28, 29, 45 (Old Greek 2:28–29); Rev 1:1 ("what must soon take place", with ἐσήμανεν, "he made known by sign" — the Old Greek's verb at 2:45); Rev 4:1 ("what must take place after these things"; Theodotion 2:29 has ἃ δεῖ γενέσθαι μετὰ ταῦτα); Rev 22:6 (λόγοι πιστοὶ καὶ ἀληθινοί, "faithful and true", with ἃ δεῖ γενέσθαι; Theodotion 2:45 ἀληθινὸν … πιστή). Suggested **high**. Test: the Greek in the Rahlfs exports and the SBLGNT; how common ἃ δεῖ γενέσθαι is elsewhere in the Greek Bible (Swete) and NT (Matt 24:6; Mark 13:7; Luke 21:9); rivals; verdict.

## A13 — Isa 41:15–16 behind Dan 2:35 (cross-language)
Stated: Isa 41:15–16 "crush (דָּקַק) … chaff (מֹץ) … the wind (רוּחַ) will carry them away (נָשָׂא)"; Dan 2:35 (Aramaic) crushed (דְּקַק) … chaff (עוּר) of the summer threshing floors … the wind (רוּחָא) carried them away (נְשָׂא). Hebrew counts: "crush" + "chaff" in one verse — Isa 41:15 only; "chaff" + "wind" + "carry" within one verse of each other — Isa 41:15–16 only. Chaff-and-wind alone is common (Ps 1:4; 35:5; Isa 17:13; Hos 13:3). Suggested **moderate–high**. Test: counts; whether the four-element sequence is decisive or the image-pool explains it (also Isa 17:13; 29:5; Job 21:18; Ps 83:14); verdict.

## A14 — Jer 27:5–7 behind Dan 2:21, 37–39 (cross-language)
Stated: Jer 27:5 ("I give it to whom it seems right to me") behind 2:21 and the book's refrain (4:17, 25, 32; 5:21); 27:6 (the land and "the beasts of the field" given to Nebuchadnezzar) behind 2:38; 27:7 ("until the time of his own land comes") behind 2:39. Rival: Jer 28:14 (beasts of the field given). Suggested **moderate–high**. Test by reading; rivals (Jer 28:14; Gen 1:28; Ps 8:7–9); verdict.

## A15 — Deut 10:17 behind Dan 2:47 (cross-language)
Stated: אֱלֹהֵי הָאֱלֹהִים וַאֲדֹנֵי הָאֲדֹנִים ("God of gods and Lord of lords", Deut 10:17; cf. Ps 136:2–3) against אֱלָהּ אֱלָהִין וּמָרֵא מַלְכִין ("a God of gods and a Lord of kings", Dan 2:47). Suggested **moderate–high**. Test: the Hebrew phrase's spread; whether "God of gods" is generic (Josh 22:22; Ps 50:1; Dan 11:36 אֵל אֵלִים); verdict.

## A16 — Arioch (Dan 2:14–25) and Gen 14:1, 9
Stated: the name אַרְיוֹךְ occurs only at Gen 14:1, 9 (king of Ellasar, ally of Amraphel king of Shinar) and Dan 2:14, 15, 24, 25. Suggested **name link high; intended allusion uncertain**. Test: count; whether the name is attested historically outside the Bible `[S]`; whether any narrative feature of Gen 14 is active in Dan 2; verdict.
