# Claims file — Auditor B (fields, designs, structures: baselines and nulls)

Daniel text: WLC (Hebrew and Aramaic); Greek: Rahlfs exports. References English numbering (Heb 3:31–4:34 = Eng 4:1–37; Heb 6:1 = Eng 5:31; Heb 6:2–29 = Eng 6:1–28).

## B1 — "The tales draw on Genesis, the visions on the Prophets"
Stated: Daniel's rare contacts with earlier Scripture divide by section: chs 1–6 draw on Genesis (Joseph, Gen 40–41: Dan 1:10; 1:20; 2:1–3; 2:30; 4:8–9, 18; 5:11, 14, 29), chs 7–12 on the Prophets (Isaiah 10:23, 25; 26:19; 53:11; 66:24; Ezekiel 1; 9–10; Habakkuk 2:3; with Numbers 24:24; Deut 32:40). A later run on ch. 2 found Isaiah 41:15–16, Jeremiah 27:5–7, Deut 10:17 and Deut 29:29 behind ch. 2 and proposed restating the claim as "the Joseph material is concentrated in the tales". Suggested **moderate (design)**. Test: build your own inventory of rare (≤ about 5 verses in the Hebrew Bible) lemma-set contacts between each Daniel chapter (Hebrew chapters only for lemma work: 1, 8–12; read the Aramaic) and Genesis vs the Prophets; a baseline — is a Genesis/Prophets split by half more than chance given where Hebrew and Aramaic fall?; verdict on the original and the restated form.

## B2 — The late Writings dominate Daniel's density scan
Stated: in the density scan (rare shared vocabulary, every chapter of the Hebrew Bible ranked), the whole-book top ten for Daniel are Ezra 5, 6, 4, 7, Neh 1, Ezra 9, Esth 1, Esth 7, Ezra 1, 2 Chr 36; for chs 8–12, 5 of the top 6 are Neh 1, Ezra 9, Esth 1, 2 Chr 36, Ezra 1 (with Ezek 9). Two explanations offered and not chosen: shared Persian-period register, or canonical design (the Writings' last books bound together). Suggested **moderate**. Test: reproduce; baseline — run the same scan on another late Hebrew book not adjacent in BHS order (e.g. Esther, Ezra–Nehemiah, Chronicles themselves; or Ecclesiastes, Jonah, Malachi) and see whether the late books dominate regardless of canonical position; verdict.

## B3 — Handoff to Ezra–Nehemiah and Chronicles
Stated: the ordered phrase אַחַת לְכוֹרֶשׁ ("the first year of Cyrus") occurs in **3 verses: Dan 1:21, Ezra 1:1, 2 Chr 36:22** — this book, the next in BHS order, and the canon's last; and Dan 9:4 hands its confession almost word for word to Neh 1:5. Proposed as a **thematic and chronological handoff; lexical form a design claim, moderate**. Test: the phrase with prefixed forms and lemma search (including Ezra 5:13; 6:3 in Aramaic, read); whether "first year of" + king formulae are common (Dan 9:1; 11:1 "first year of Darius"); the Neh 1:5 comparison against Deut 7:9, 1 Kgs 8:23, Neh 9:32; verdict.

## B4 — The Aramaic chapters as a concentric arrangement 2 // 7, 3 // 6, 4 // 5
Stated: proposed in scholarship `[S]`; tested once by comparing the claimed pairing against all 15 ways of pairing six chapters, scoring by shared vocabulary (words common to every Aramaic chapter set aside): first of 15 on three of four measures (overall overlap; vocabulary shared only by the two chapters of a pair; each on Hebrew and English chapter divisions), second on the fourth; margins thin (0.654 vs 0.645 on one); 4 // 5 and 3 // 6 well supported, 2 // 7 weakest lexically. Exclusive evidence offered: "He sent his angel" (שְׁלַח מַלְאֲכֵהּ) at 3:28 and 6:22 [Heb 6:23]; שֵׁיזִב ("deliver") only in chs 3 and 6 (9 occurrences in 7 verses); the four verses with שַׁלִּיט + מַלְכוּ + צְבָא (4:17, 25, 32; 5:21). A later run added: דְּקַק ("crush") used of the fourth kingdom at 2:40 and the fourth beast at 7:7, 19, 23. Suggested **moderate**. Test: reproduce the exclusive items; repeat a baseline of your own design (e.g. permutation, or score by rare lemmas only, or by theme-blind content words); are linear readings (2→3→4→5→6→7) or other pairings as well supported?; verdict.

## B5 — Daniel follows Genesis 40–41 in sequence across chs 1–5
Stated: Daniel moves through the Joseph story in order — the prison chapter (Gen 40, "dejected" faces, 40:6) at Dan 1:10; the palace chapter (Gen 41) at Dan 2; the robe and gold chain (Gen 41:42) at Dan 5:29 (cross-language: "clothed in purple", "chain of gold on his neck"). Suggested **moderate–high (synthetic; every constituent verified; design not baselined)**. Test: is the order real (do Gen 41 contacts also appear in Dan 1, or Gen 40 in Dan 2/4/5?); how many Joseph contacts are there in all, and where; baseline — would any two-chapter source cited three times in a five-chapter span appear "in order" by chance (1 in 2 for two points, 1 in 6 for three)?; verdict.

## B6 — Daniel 2:20–23 (the doxology) as the key of Daniel 2
Stated: each line of the hymn is answered later in ch. 2 by the same words (WLC): "changes times" (שְׁנָה + עִדָּן, 2:21) ↔ the Chaldeans "until the time changes" (2:9); "sets up kings" (קוּם, 2:21) ↔ kingdoms "arise" and God "sets up" his kingdom (2:39, 44); "gives wisdom" (יְהַב + חָכְמָה, 2:21, 23) ↔ "not by any wisdom in me" (2:30) and "has given you the kingdom" (2:37–38); "reveals deep things" (גְּלָה, 2:22) ↔ 2:28, 29, 47. Also proposed: an inclusio "all the wise men of Babylon" (2:12 → 2:48), and the antithesis "there is not (לָא אִיתַי) a man on earth" (2:10) / "there is (אִיתַי) a God in heaven" (2:28). Suggested **high on the links; moderate–high on the design**. Test: verify each lemma link; baseline — take any four-verse stretch of Dan 2 (or of chs 3–6) and count how many of its content lemmas recur elsewhere in the chapter: is the hymn's recurrence rate unusual?; verdict.
