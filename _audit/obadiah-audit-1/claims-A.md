# Claims for Auditor A — single contacts (wording, rarity, rivals, Greek)

Hebrew counts as stated are for the WLC. References follow Hebrew versification.

**A1. Joel 3:5 at Obad 17.** פְּלֵיטָה ("those who escape") with צִיּוֹן ("Zion") and הַר ("mount") in one verse: four verses in the Hebrew Bible, 2 Kgs 19:31, Isa 37:32, Joel 3:5, Obad 17. Joel is the closest, not verbatim. Joel's כַּאֲשֶׁר אָמַר יְהוָה ("as the LORD has said") shows a prior word but not whose. Also פְּלֵיטָה with שָׂרִיד ("survivor") in one verse: Joel 3:5 only; Obadiah has שָׂרִיד at v. 18.

**A2. Joel 4:3, 4, 7, 14, 17, 19 at Obad 11, 15, 17, 10.** יַדּוּ גוֹרָל ("cast lots"): Joel 4:3, Obad 11, Nah 3:10 only. גְּמוּל ("recompense") with רֹאשׁ ("head") and שׁוּב ("return"): Joel 4:4, 7 and Obad 15 only. חָמָס ("violence") with אֱדוֹם ("Edom") in one verse: Joel 4:19 only (Obadiah has them apart). זָר ("stranger") with יְרוּשָׁלִַם ("Jerusalem") in one verse: Joel 4:17 and Obad 11 only. Claimed: Joel is a live partner of Obadiah.

**A3. Ps 22:29 at Obad 21.** The ordered phrase לַיהוָה הַמְּלוּכָה ("to the LORD the kingdom") stands in those two verses only.

**A4. Ps 41:10 and Jer 38:22 at Obad 7.** אִישׁ ("man"), שָׁלוֹם ("peace") and לֶחֶם ("bread") in one verse: Ps 41:10 and Obad 7 only. אַנְשֵׁי שְׁלֹמֶךָ ("men at peace with you") with יָכֹל ("prevail"): Jer 38:22 and Obad 7 only.

**A5. Gen 27:41 at Obad 3, 10, 15.** וַיֹּאמֶר עֵשָׂו בְּלִבּוֹ ("And Esau said in his heart") with יִקְרְבוּ יְמֵי ("the days… are near") and יַעֲקֹב אָחִי ("my brother Jacob"), against Obad 3 אֹמֵר בְּלִבּוֹ ("who say in your heart"), Obad 10 אָחִיךָ יַעֲקֹב ("your brother Jacob"), Obad 15 קָרוֹב יוֹם ("the day draws near"). אָמַר, לֵב and עֵשָׂו in one verse: Gen 27:41 only. Claimed: Obadiah recalls this verse. Test especially whether "said in his heart" is idiom, and whether קָרַב (verb) and קָרוֹב (adjective) can bear this.

**A6. Deut 32:35 at Obad 13, 15.** כִּי קָרוֹב יוֹם אֵידָם ("for the day of their calamity is near"). קָרוֹב ("near"), יוֹם ("day") and אֵיד ("calamity") in one verse in this sense: Deut 32:35 only (Prov 27:10 has קָרוֹב in another sense). Obad 13 has בְּיוֹם אֵידָם ("in the day of their disaster"), Obad 15 כִּי־קָרוֹב יוֹם־יְהוָה ("for the day of the LORD draws near"). In Greek: Deut 32:35 Rahlfs ἀνταποδώσω… ὅτι ἐγγὺς ἡμέρα ἀπωλείας αὐτῶν against Obad 12 ἡμέρᾳ ἀπωλείας αὐτῶν and Obad 15 ἐγγὺς ἡμέρα… τὸ ἀνταπόδομά… ἀνταποδοθήσεται. Claimed: moderate to high, and that Rom 12:19's quotation of this line bears on how Obad 15 is used. Test rivals (Jer 18:17; 46:21; Ezek 35:5; the many "near day" verses).

**A7. Prov 27:10 at Obad 10–13.** אָח ("brother"), בּוֹא ("enter") and אֵיד ("calamity") in one verse: Prov 27:10 only; it has אַל־תָּבוֹא ("do not go/enter") with בְּיוֹם אֵידֶךָ ("in the day of your calamity") and מֵאָח רָחוֹק ("than a brother far away"), against Obad 13 אַל־תָּבוֹא… בְּיוֹם אֵידָם and Obad 11 מִנֶּגֶד ("aloof"). Note the sense is different (in Proverbs, one should not go to a brother). Also Prov 24:17 אַל־תִּשְׂמַח ("do not rejoice") and Prov 24:29, and the claim that vv. 12–14 have a wisdom register.

**A8. Mic 7:8–10 at Obad 10, 12.** אַל־תִּשְׂמְחִי ("do not rejoice"), וּתְכַסֶּהָ בוּשָׁה ("and shame will cover her"). כָּסָה ("cover") with בּוּשָׁה ("shame"): Ezek 7:18, Mic 7:10, Obad 10 only.

**A9. Hab 2:9, 16, 17 at Obad 3–4, 10, 16.** מָרוֹם ("height") with קֵן ("nest"): Jer 49:16 and Hab 2:9 only (Obadiah has them across vv. 3–4). שְׁתֵה גַם־אַתָּה ("drink, you too", 2:16). חֲמַס לְבָנוֹן יְכַסֶּךָּ ("the violence… will overwhelm you", 2:17): חָמָס with כָּסָה, seven verses.

**A10. Ps 7:17 at Obad 4, 10, 15.** שׁוּב ("return"), רֹאשׁ ("head"), חָמָס ("violence") and יָרַד ("descend") in one verse: Ps 7:17 only. Rivals to test: Judg 1:7; Lev 24:19; Jer 50:15, 29; Ezek 35:15; 1 Kgs 2:33; Ps 94:23.

**A11. Lam 2:3, 22; 4:21–22 at Obad 18, 14, 12.** יַעֲקֹב ("Jacob") with לֶהָבָה ("flame"): Lam 2:3 and Obad 18 only. Claimed: fire that burned in Jacob becomes Jacob as fire. פָּלִיט ("fugitive") with שָׂרִיד ("survivor"): five verses including Lam 2:22 and Obad 14. שָׂמַח ("rejoice") with Edom in one verse: Lam 4:21 only.

**A12. Sound plays in Obad 11–13.** אֵידָם ("their disaster", ʾêdām) against אֱדוֹם ("Edom", ʾĕdôm), with Ezek 35:5 בְּעֵת אֵידָם as support. נָכְרוֹ ("his misfortune", v. 12) against נָכְרִים ("foreigners", v. 11), with the Greek ἀλλοτρίων/ἀλλότριοι. Test how often אֵיד and אֱדוֹם share a context, and whether the second is a sound play or a reading.
