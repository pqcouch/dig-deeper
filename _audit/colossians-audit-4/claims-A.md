# Claims file — Auditor A (single contacts: wording, rarity, rivals, Greek and Hebrew)

Each claim is stated as the proposing reports state it, counts included. Test each. Colossians text: NA28 export and SBLGNT.

## A1 — Job 31:13–15 behind Colossians 4:1 [queue C44]
- Col 4:1 Οἱ κύριοι, τὸ δίκαιον καὶ τὴν ἰσότητα τοῖς δούλοις παρέχεσθε, εἰδότες ὅτι καὶ ὑμεῖς ἔχετε κύριον ἐν οὐρανῷ ("Masters, grant your slaves justice and fairness, knowing that you too have a Master in heaven").
- Stated: Job 31:13–15 — Job's oath that he did not despise the cause of his male or female slave when they contended with him, "for what shall I do when God rises up … did not he who made me in the womb make him?" — the master answerable to God for his slave, one Maker. Proposed as closer than Lev 25:43, 53 (whose Greek is ἐν τῷ μόχθῳ, "with toil"). Suggested **low–moderate**.
- Test: the Greek of Job 31:13–15 (Swete and Rahlfs) and the Hebrew (WLC); shared wording with Col 4:1 (δοῦλος/παῖς, θεράπαινα, κύριος, δίκαιον, κρίσις/κρίνω); rivals (Lev 25:43, 46, 53; Deut 15; Sir 7:20–21; 33:31; Eph 6:9; Exod 21); whether "one Maker" is in the Greek; how often the master-answerable-to-God idea is expressed in Swete. Verdict.

## A2 — 1 Kingdoms 12:24 (12:14) and Psalm 18:8–14 LXX behind Colossians 3:22–24 [C45]
- Col 3:22 … ἐν ἁπλότητι καρδίας φοβούμενοι τὸν κύριον; 3:23 ὃ ἐὰν ποιῆτε, ἐκ ψυχῆς ἐργάζεσθε ὡς τῷ κυρίῳ; 3:24 εἰδότες ὅτι ἀπὸ κυρίου ἀπολήμψεσθε τὴν ἀνταπόδοσιν τῆς κληρονομίας· τῷ κυρίῳ Χριστῷ δουλεύετε.
- Stated: 1 Rgns 12:24 (and 12:14) "fear the Lord and serve him … with all your heart" — a formula of fearing and serving the Lord with the whole heart (moderate as formula). Ps 18:8–14 LXX (Ps 19 EVV) — the fear of the Lord, "your servant" (ὁ δοῦλός σου), and ἀνταπόδοσις πολλή ("great reward", 18:12) — **the only Swete passage with reward (ἀνταπόδοσις) + servant + fear within four verses**; ἀνταπόδοσις occurs only here in the NT (Col 3:24). Suggested **moderate (formula); low–moderate (Ps 18 behind the hapax)**.
- Test: counts (fear + serve + heart in Swete; ἀνταπόδοσις with δοῦλος and φόβος/φοβέομαι within four verses — reproduce "only"); other ἀνταπόδοσις verses in Swete and their sense (recompense as punishment vs reward); rivals (Deut 6:13; 10:12, 20; Josh 24:14; Isa 40:10; 62:11; Ps 102 LXX); whether the NT hapax is merely Paul's ordinary word. Verdict.

## A3 — "The lot of the holy ones in light" (Col 1:12) [C46]
- Col 1:12 εὐχαριστοῦντες τῷ πατρὶ τῷ ἱκανώσαντι ὑμᾶς εἰς τὴν μερίδα τοῦ κλήρου τῶν ἁγίων ἐν τῷ φωτί.
- Stated: Wis 5:5 ("how was he counted among the sons of God, and his lot (κλῆρος) is among the holy ones?") is **the only Swete verse joining κλῆρος with "the holy ones" in this sense** (1 Chr 24:5 and Neh 11:1 have the two words otherwise); OG Dan 7:18–27 (the holy ones of the Most High receive the kingdom); Acts 26:18 (κλῆρον ἐν τοῖς ἡγιασμένοις, with "darkness … light", "the authority of Satan", "forgiveness") **shares six rarer lemmas with Col 1:12–14 in one verse (the next NT rival has four)**; Qumran "lot of the holy ones" [S]. The saints-as-believers reading (not angels) is favoured on the letter's usage — every other plural ἅγιοι in Colossians = believers. Suggested **moderate**.
- Test: counts (κληρ- with ἁγι- in Swete; the Acts 26:18 overlap and its baseline against other NT verses); the referent of οἱ ἅγιοι (angels vs saints) in Wis 5:5, Dan 7, and Colossians (count plural ἅγιοι in the letter); whether μερίς + κλῆρος is a stock doublet (reported as 20 Swete verses) that makes the phrase idiom. Verdict on (a) Wis 5:5, (b) Dan 7, (c) Acts 26:18 as a Pauline/Lukan parallel, (d) the saints-not-angels reading.

## A4 — Exodus 6:6–8 behind Colossians 1:12–14 [C48]
- Col 1:13 ὃς ἐρρύσατο ἡμᾶς ἐκ τῆς ἐξουσίας τοῦ σκότους καὶ μετέστησεν …; 1:14 ἐν ᾧ ἔχομεν τὴν ἀπολύτρωσιν.
- Stated: Exod 6:6–8 — ῥύσομαι ὑμᾶς ἐκ τῆς δουλείας … λυτρώσομαι ὑμᾶς … (6:6); the land given ἐν κλήρῳ (6:8). ῥύομαι + λυτρόω together in one verse in **3 Swete verses: Exod 6:6, Ps 68:19, Hos 13:14; only Exod 6 adds κλῆρος**. The NT scan cannot see it (stem changes). Suggested **moderate**.
- Test: reproduce the counts with augmented forms (ἐρρύσατο, ἐλυτρώσατο) — stem anywhere; ἀπολύτρωσις vs λυτρόω (cognate, different word); κλῆρος at Exod 6:8 (Swete and Rahlfs); rivals (Deut 7:8; 9:26; 13:5; Ps 105 LXX; Isa 44; Exod 15:13 "redeemed … holy habitation"); whether "transferred into the kingdom" and "darkness" fit an exodus reading (Wis 17–18 plague of darkness is a separate claim for Auditor B; note but do not judge it). Verdict.

## A5 — Deuteronomy 29:29 (Swete 29:28) behind Colossians 3:3–4 [C52]
- Col 3:3 ἡ ζωὴ ὑμῶν κέκρυπται σὺν τῷ Χριστῷ ἐν τῷ θεῷ· 3:4 ὅταν ὁ Χριστὸς φανερωθῇ … τότε καὶ ὑμεῖς σὺν αὐτῷ φανερωθήσεσθε ἐν δόξῃ.
- Stated: Deut 29:29 EVV — "the secret things (τὰ κρυπτά) belong to the LORD our God, but the things revealed (τὰ φανερά) belong to us" — **the only Swete verse with both a κρυπτ- and a φανερ- word (floor: reduplicated perfects missed)**. Colossians inverts it: the believers' life hidden in God, to be revealed. Rival: Mark 4:22 / Luke 8:17 (the same pair in a saying of Jesus). Suggested **low–moderate**.
- Test: Swete verse numbering for this verse; the count with stem-anywhere searches (κεκρυμ-, ἐκρυβ-, ἐφανερ-, ἀπόκρυφ-), positive control; rivals (Sir 1:30; 4:18; Isa 45:3; Dan 2:22; Wis 7:21; Prov 2; Matt 10:26); NT κρυπτ- + φανερ- verses (Rom 2:16, 28–29; 1 Cor 4:5; 14:25; Mark 4:22; Luke 8:17); whether Colossians' pair (hidden life, revealed in glory) is closer to Deuteronomy's (secret things / revealed things of the law) or to the Jesus saying or to Paul's own 1 Cor 4:5. Verdict.

## A6 — Deuteronomy 7:6–8 (with 4:37; 10:15) behind "chosen of God, holy and beloved" (Colossians 3:12) [C55]
- Col 3:12 Ἐνδύσασθε οὖν, ὡς ἐκλεκτοὶ τοῦ θεοῦ ἅγιοι καὶ ἠγαπημένοι.
- Stated: Deut 7:6–8 — λαὸς ἅγιος (7:6) … προείλατο … ἐξελέξατο (7:6–7) … παρὰ τὸ ἀγαπᾶν Κύριον ὑμᾶς (7:8); ἐξελε- with ἀγαπ- in one verse also at Deut 4:37 and 10:15 (Swete). Deut 7 joins the titles to putting away idolatry (7:1–5; cf. Col 3:5 "greed, which is idolatry"). Rivals named: 2 Macc 1:25 (ἐκλεκτ- + ἁγι-), Isa 65:9, 1 Thess 1:4 ("beloved by God … your election"). Deuteronomy has the verbs, not the adjective ἐκλεκτός. ἐκλεκτός + ἅγιος + ἀγαπάω together: Col 3:12 only in the NT. Suggested **moderate (idea); low–moderate (Deut 7 specifically)**.
- Test: counts (ἐκλεκτ-/ἐκλεγ-/ἐξελε- with ἁγι- and ἀγαπ- within three verses in Swete — is Deut 7:6–8 the densest?); positive controls for augmented ἐξελέξατο; rivals (Isa 41:8–9; 43:20; 44:1–2 "Jacob my servant, the beloved (ἠγαπημένος) Israel whom I chose"; Ps 104 LXX; 1 Pet 2:9; Sir 46; Pss Sol); whether the triple is better explained as Paul's own election vocabulary (Rom 1:7 ἀγαπητοῖς θεοῦ, κλητοῖς ἁγίοις; 1 Thess 1:4; 2 Thess 2:13). Verdict.
