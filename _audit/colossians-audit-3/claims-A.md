# Claims file — Auditor A (single contacts: wording, rarity, rivals, Greek and Hebrew)

Each claim is stated as the proposing reports state it, counts included. Test each. Colossians text: NA28 export and SBLGNT.

## A1 — Wisdom 7:17 behind Colossians 2:8, 20 (and 1:17) [queue C29]
- Col 2:8 κατὰ τὰ στοιχεῖα τοῦ κόσμου; 2:20 ἀπὸ τῶν στοιχείων τοῦ κόσμου; 1:17 καὶ τὰ πάντα ἐν αὐτῷ συνέστηκεν.
- Stated: Wis 7:17, εἰδέναι σύστασιν κόσμου καὶ ἐνέργειαν στοιχείων ("to know the structure of the world and the working of the elements"), is **the only Swete verse joining στοιχεῖον ("element") with κόσμος ("world")**, the pair of Col 2:8 and 2:20. σύστασις ("structure") is cognate with συνίστημι, "hold together" (συνέστηκεν, 1:17). στοιχει-, stem anywhere, occurs in Swete at Wis 7:17, 19:18, 2 Macc 7:22, 4 Macc 12:13. Wisdom 7 ranks 1st against Gal 4:3–8 in a scan. Suggested **low–moderate to moderate** (Swete only).
- Test: counts (including στοιχει- with κοσμ- within one or two verses); the sense of στοιχεῖον in each hit; whether Wis 7:17's "elements" (physical, as objects of knowledge given by God) fits Colossians' use (a regime from which one "died", 2:20); whether συνίστημι/σύστασις is a real link or a false friend; Gal 4:3, 9 as the nearer Pauline use. Verdict.

## A2 — Isaiah 29:14 behind Colossians 2:2–3, as an inversion [C30]
- Col 2:2–3 … εἰς ἐπίγνωσιν τοῦ μυστηρίου τοῦ θεοῦ, Χριστοῦ, ἐν ᾧ εἰσιν πάντες οἱ θησαυροὶ τῆς σοφίας καὶ γνώσεως ἀπόκρυφοι; 2:2 also has τῆς συνέσεως.
- Stated: Isa 29:14 (ἀπολῶ τὴν σοφίαν τῶν σοφῶν, καὶ τὴν σύνεσιν τῶν συνετῶν κρύψω, "I will destroy the wisdom of the wise and hide the understanding of the prudent") is **the only Swete verse with σοφία ("wisdom"), σύνεσις ("understanding") and a hiding word**; **Col 2:2–3 is the only NT window with the same three**; 1 Cor 2:7 is the only other NT verse with σοφία and a hiding word (θεοῦ σοφίαν ἐν μυστηρίῳ, τὴν ἀποκεκρυμμένην). Paul quotes Isa 29:14 at 1 Cor 1:19 and goes on to 1 Cor 2:7. The Jesus tradition reads the verse as a hiding text (Matt 11:25; Luke 10:21: "you hid these things from the wise and understanding"). **Claim:** Col 2:2–3 inverts Isa 29:14 — God hid understanding *from* the wise; now all treasures of wisdom are hidden *in* Christ and disclosed. Caveat: Paul's 1 Cor 1:19 reads ἀθετήσω ("I will set aside"), not κρύψω. Within one verse either side, the triple also occurs at Prov 2:2–4, Sir 1:25, Sir 14:20, Sir 39:6 and Theodotion Dan 2:20–23. Suggested **moderate** (provisional).
- Test: counts (NT by lemma; Swete by stem anywhere, sense-checked); rivals (Prov 2; Theodotion Dan 2; Isa 45:3) — does any of them fit 2:2–3 better? Does the 1 Cor 1:19 → 2:7 route actually carry a *hiding* reading of Isa 29:14, given ἀθετήσω? Are Matt 11:25 / Luke 10:21 relevant to Paul? Verdict.

## A3 — Psalm 50:8 LXX (Hebrew 51:6) at Colossians 1:26–27; 2:3 [C33]
- Stated: Ps 50:8 LXX, τὰ ἄδηλα καὶ τὰ κρύφια τῆς σοφίας σου ἐδήλωσάς μοι ("the hidden and secret things of your wisdom you made known to me"), joins hidden things, wisdom and disclosure, as Col 1:26–27 (τὸ μυστήριον τὸ ἀποκεκρυμμένον … νῦν δὲ ἐφανερώθη … γνωρίσαι) and 2:3 do. Suggested **low**.
- Test: wording (Swete, Rahlfs); the Hebrew of 51:8 (Hebrew numbering; the LXX's "hidden things" — is it in the Hebrew, בַּסָּתֻם, or the translator's?); shared words with Col 1:26–27 and 2:3 (count them); rivals with the same hidden-wisdom-disclosed shape (Dan 2:22, 28 Theod.; Sir 1:6; 39:3; Wis 6:22). Verdict.

## A4 — 2 Chronicles 19:6–7 (with Deuteronomy 10:17) behind Colossians 3:22–25; 4:1 [C35]
- Col 3:22–25 (read it in the SBLGNT and NA28 export); 4:1 οἱ κύριοι, τὸ δίκαιον καὶ τὴν ἰσότητα τοῖς δούλοις παρέχεσθε, εἰδότες ὅτι καὶ ὑμεῖς ἔχετε κύριον ἐν οὐρανῷ.
- Stated: 2 Chr 19:6–7 (Jehoshaphat's charge to judges): "you do not judge for man but for the LORD (οὐκ ἀνθρώπῳ … ἀλλ᾽ ἢ τῷ κυρίῳ) … let the fear of the LORD (φόβος κυρίου) be upon you … there is with the LORD our God no unrighteousness (ἀδικία) nor partiality (θαυμάσαι πρόσωπον)". Counts stated:
  - a fear-word, κύριος, an ἀδικ- word and a πρόσωπον idiom in one Swete verse: **2 Chr 19:7 only**;
  - with an ἄνθρωπ- word added, within two verses either side: 2 Chr 19:5–8, Isa 29:21–23, Job 11:13–14, Prov 29:25–27, Jer 40:7, Jdt 11:3 — only 2 Chr 19 names partiality as a judge's sin;
  - "not for man … but for the Lord" (οὐκ ἀνθρώπῳ … κυρίῳ): 1 Chr 29:1 and 2 Chr 19:6 only (Swete); Rahlfs agrees;
  - 1 Chr 29:17 has ἐν ἁπλότητι καρδίας ("in sincerity of heart"), the phrase of Col 3:22.
  - Deut 10:17: "the LORD your God … Lord of lords (κύριος τῶν κυρίων) … who does not show partiality (οὐ θαυμάζει πρόσωπον) nor take a bribe"; the Hebrew of both Deut 10:17 and 2 Chr 19:7 has פָּנִים ("face") with שֹׁחַד ("bribe"); "Lord of lords" without partiality is the background of 4:1.
- Caveat stated by the proposer: Eph 6:5–9 has the same elements, so a shared Christian tradition explains it equally well; Paul does not quote Chronicles. Suggested **low–moderate**.
- Test: every count (Swete stem-anywhere, sense-checked; Rahlfs for Chronicles and Deuteronomy; WLC lemma for the Hebrew); rivals for impartial-judge-plus-fear language (Lev 19:15; Deut 1:17; 16:19; Sir 35:12–13 (Swete numbering?); Job 34:19; Rom 2:11; Acts 10:34); is ἁπλότης καρδίας common? Verdict.

## A5 — Leviticus 25:39–55 behind Colossians 3:22; 4:1 [C36]
- Stated: Lev 25:39–43, 55: the Israelite debt-slave is not to be made to serve as a slave; "they are My servants (οἰκέται μου) whom I brought out of Egypt"; "you shall not rule over him with harshness (Hebrew בְּפָרֶךְ, the word of Exod 1:13–14) but you shall fear your God (Swete: φοβηθήσῃ κύριον τὸν θεόν σου; Hebrew 'your God')". Two ideas meet Colossians: the fear of God as the master's restraint (given to the slave in 3:22); the slaves belong to another Master (Lev 25:42, 55; Col 4:1). Leviticus limits this to Israelites (25:44–46). No shared phrase. Suggested **low–moderate (conceptual)**.
- Test: wording (Swete, Rahlfs, WLC); counts of פֶּרֶךְ (WLC lemma); any verbal contact with Col 3:22–4:1 at all; rivals (Deut 15:12–18; Exod 21; Jer 34; Neh 5; Job 31:13–15 — the master and slave made by one Maker). Is Job 31:13–15 closer to 4:1? Verdict.

## A6 — Isaiah 54:17 (Hebrew) behind Colossians 3:24 [C37]
- Col 3:24 εἰδότες ὅτι ἀπὸ κυρίου ἀπολήμψεσθε τὴν ἀνταπόδοσιν τῆς κληρονομίας· τῷ κυρίῳ Χριστῷ δουλεύετε.
- Stated: Isa 54:17 זֹאת נַחֲלַת עַבְדֵי יְהוָה ("this is the heritage of the servants of YHWH") is **the only verse in the Hebrew Bible where an inheritance belongs to "the servants of YHWH", plural**; נַחֲלָה with עֶבֶד in one verse: twelve verses (WLC). Swete renders ἔστιν κληρονομία τοῖς θεραπεύουσιν κύριον ("an inheritance for those who serve the Lord"), with θεραπεύω, not δοῦλος/δουλεύω — so Col 3:24's δουλεύετε is closer to the Hebrew than to the Greek. Isa 63:17 and 65:9 have δοῦλοι with κληρονομ- (Swete); Isa 63:4 has ἀνταπόδοσις. Paul quotes Isa 54:1 at Gal 4:27, in the slave woman / free woman argument that ends "the son of the slave woman shall not inherit" (Gal 4:30). Suggested **low–moderate**.
- Test: the twelve-verse count and the "only plural" claim (WLC lemma; read each); Swete and Rahlfs at 54:17; δουλ- with κληρονομ- in one verse across Swete (rivals); ἀνταπόδοσις with κληρονομία in one verse anywhere in Swete; does the Galatians use really raise availability? Verdict.

## A7 — Deuteronomy 21:18–21 reversed at Colossians 3:20–21 [C38]
- Col 3:20 τὰ τέκνα, ὑπακούετε τοῖς γονεῦσιν; 3:21 οἱ πατέρες, μὴ ἐρεθίζετε τὰ τέκνα ὑμῶν.
- Stated: the ἐρεθ- family ("provoke") with ὑπακούω ("obey") occurs in Swete **only at Deut 21:18 and 21:20** (the stubborn and rebellious son, οὐχ ὑπακούων); in Deuteronomy the son provokes and disobeys, in Colossians the child obeys and the father must not provoke — a reversal. Deuteronomy 21 ranks first in two of three rare-word scan runs (median 11). Suggested **low**.
- Test: the counts (ἐρεθ- stem anywhere — note Swete's ἐρεθιστής; sense-check); is ἐρεθ- in Deut 21 the *son's* provoking (active) or something else? Eph 6:4 has παροργίζετε (and the NA28 apparatus at Col 3:21 a variant παροργίζετε) — does that weaken or strengthen? Verdict.

## A8 — The Joshua 18–19 allotment field behind Colossians 1:12 and 2:18 [C43]
- Col 1:12 … τῷ ἱκανώσαντι ὑμᾶς εἰς τὴν μερίδα τοῦ κλήρου τῶν ἁγίων ἐν τῷ φωτί; 2:18 … ἃ ἑόρακεν ἐμβατεύων.
- Stated: ἐμβατεύω ("enter, take possession") occurs in Swete in seven verses — Josh 19:49, 51; 1 Macc 12:25; 13:20; 14:31; 15:40; 2 Macc 2:30. Josh 19:49 has ἐμβατεῦσαι with κλῆρον; μερίς ("portion") and κλῆρος ("lot") stand together in the allotment chapters (e.g. Josh 18:6–7; 19:9). Commentators (recalled) bring the Joshua allotment to both 1:12 and 2:18. **But no Swete verse has ἐμβατεύω, μερίς and κλῆρος together.** Suggested **low–moderate**.
- Test: counts; μερίς with κλῆρος in one verse across Swete (how many? is it a common pairing, e.g. Deut 10:9; 12:12; 14:27, 29; Num 18:20?); the Hebrew behind μερίς τοῦ κλήρου (חֵלֶק + גּוֹרָל / נַחֲלָה); does 1:12's phrase point to Joshua in particular or to the Levites' "portion and lot" (Num 18:20; Deut 10:9) — which is closer? Does the ἐμβατεύω link at 2:18 survive the sense test (entering land vs entering visions)? Verdict.
