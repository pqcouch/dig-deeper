# Claims file — Auditor B (field and design: block tests, chance baselines, rank stability, leave-one-out)

Each claim is stated as the proposing reports state it, counts included. Test each. Colossians text: NA28 export and SBLGNT.

## B1 — Wisdom 14 as the polemical partner of Colossians 2:16–23 [queue C31]
- Stated: Wisdom 14 shares with Col 2:16–23 pejorative θρησκεία/θρησκεύω ("worship", Wis 14:16, 18, 27; Col 2:18, and ἐθελοθρησκία 2:23); κρύφια μυστήρια ("secret mysteries", 14:23; cf. Col 1:26–27; 2:2); a custom kept as law "by the commands of tyrants" (ἐπιταγαῖς, 14:16; cf. δόγματα, 2:14, 20; ἐντάλματα, 2:22); the human origin of false worship. In a six-run scan of Col 2:16–23 against Swete, Wisdom 14 has a **median rank of 2** (with 2 Macc 7 also at 2, Isa 1 at 4, 4 Macc 5 at 5); stated to be rare in a null of comparable passages. θρησκ-, stem anywhere, in Swete: Wis 11:15; 14:16, 18, 27; 4 Macc 5:6, 13. Suggested **moderate** (Swete only; no Rahlfs Wisdom in the corpus).
- Test: reproduce the six-run ranks; leave-one-out (does Wisdom 14 survive without θρησκ-? without μυστήρ-?); a null of 30+ comparable Pauline passages — how often does a single Swete chapter reach median ≤2?; sense-check every matched stem; is the shared vocabulary polemic against *idolatry* (Wis 14) a fair match for polemic against a Jewish-ascetic regime (Col 2)? Verdict.

## B2 — Isaiah 1:13–14 at Colossians 2:16 and 2:23 [C32]
- Col 2:16 … ἐν μέρει ἑορτῆς ἢ νεομηνίας ἢ σαββάτων; 2:23 … πρὸς πλησμονὴν τῆς σαρκός.
- Stated: νουμηνία, σάββατα and ἑορτή in one Swete verse: 2 Chr 2:4; 31:3; 1 Esd 5:51; Neh 10:33; Hos 2:11; Ezek 45:17; 1 Macc 10:34. Isa 1:14 has νουμηνίας, ἑορτάς and εἰς πλησμονήν ("a surfeit": "your new moons and feasts my soul hates; you have become a surfeit to me") — **the only Swete verse where πλησμονή meets the calendar** (Rahlfs agrees). The Hebrew of 1:14 has לָטֹרַח ("a burden"), so the πλησμονή contact exists only in the Greek. Isaiah 1 ranks median 4 in the six-run scan of Col 2:16–23. The calendar triad is formulaic (Hos 2:11 judgement; Ezek 45:17 eschatological). Suggested **moderate**; "the πλησμονή contact is the test".
- Test: counts; πλησμονή in Swete (how many verses? is "surfeit" a natural rendering of a calendar critique elsewhere?); the sense of πλησμονή in Col 2:23 (gratification of the flesh) against Isa 1:14 (God sated with offerings) — real link or false friend?; rank stability and leave-one-out (Isaiah 1 without πλησμονή; without the calendar words); is Isa 1:13–14 closer to Col 2:16 than Hos 2:11 or Ezek 45:17? Verdict.

## B3 — The ἀρεσκ- family 1:10 → 3:20 → 3:22 as designed [C39]
- Col 1:10 περιπατῆσαι ἀξίως τοῦ κυρίου εἰς πᾶσαν ἀρεσκείαν; 3:20 τοῦτο γὰρ εὐάρεστόν ἐστιν ἐν κυρίῳ; 3:22 μὴ ἐν ὀφθαλμοδουλίαις, ὡς ἀνθρωπάρεσκοι.
- Stated: the ἀρεσκ- family's only three members in Colossians, each once; ἀρεσκεία is an NT hapax; "in everything" (κατὰ πάντα, 3:20, 22; and πᾶσαν, 1:10). Eph 5:10 (εὐάρεστον τῷ κυρίῳ) and 6:6 (ὡς ἀνθρωπάρεσκοι) also pair the words. Claim: the code answers the opening prayer — please the Lord in everything — by design. Suggested **moderate**.
- Test: counts (SBLGNT lemma: ἀρεσκεία, ἀρέσκω, εὐάρεστος, εὐαρεστέω, ἀνθρωπάρεσκος; across Paul); baseline: for a Pauline letter, how often does a word family in an opening prayer recur in the ethical section? (sample other letters' opening prayers and their families); does Ephesians' parallel strengthen design or show a shared idiom? Verdict.

## B4 — Colossians 1:12 κλῆρος → 3:24 κληρονομία: the slaves among the heirs [C40]
- Col 1:12 εἰς τὴν μερίδα τοῦ κλήρου τῶν ἁγίων; 3:24 τὴν ἀνταπόδοσιν τῆς κληρονομίας.
- Stated: κληρονομία occurs once in Colossians (3:24) and κλῆρος once (1:12); cognates; the opening thanksgiving says the Father made the readers heirs, and the code tells the slaves they are among them. ἀνταπόδοσις is an NT hapax. Eph 6:8 has no inheritance (only "receive back"). Suggested **moderate–high**.
- Test: counts (SBLGNT lemma; κληρ- family in Colossians and Ephesians — note Eph 1:11, 14, 18; 5:5); is the link *design* or simply Paul's normal inheritance vocabulary? Baseline: how often do κλῆρος/κληρονομία-family words recur across a Pauline letter's opening and close? Is 1:12 → 3:24 more specific than 1:12 → (any) inheritance language? Sense: "share of the lot of the saints in light" (1:12) and "reward consisting of the inheritance" (3:24). Verdict.

## B5 — The household code as 3:17 applied [C41]
- Col 3:17 καὶ πᾶν ὅ τι ἐὰν ποιῆτε ἐν λόγῳ ἢ ἐν ἔργῳ, πάντα ἐν ὀνόματι κυρίου Ἰησοῦ; 3:23 ὃ ἐὰν ποιῆτε, ἐκ ψυχῆς ἐργάζεσθε, ὡς τῷ κυρίῳ.
- Stated: "whatever you do" (ὃ / ὅ τι ἐὰν ποιῆτε) occurs in the NT **only at Col 3:17 and 3:23**; the code (3:18–4:1) begins asyndetically at 3:18; κύριος occurs 9× in 6 of its 9 verses; so the code is 3:17's "everything in the name of the Lord" applied to the household. Words: high; function: moderate–high.
- Test: the phrase count (SBLGNT; check the NA28 apparatus at 3:17 and 3:23 for ὅ τι / ὃ and ἐάν/ἄν variants; check 1 Cor 10:31 εἴτε … ποιεῖτε, πάντα; Matt 7:12; Luke 10:35; John 2:5); the κύριος count in 3:18–4:1 and in the letter (lemma; note "κύριος" for human masters); asyndeton at 3:18 — does it bind or separate?; Ephesians' code begins with 5:21 (ὑποτασσόμενοι) — a participle tied to 5:18 — does that comparison favour the claim? Verdict.

## B6 — "Fearing the Lord" (3:22) as the LXX formula directed to Christ [C42]
- Col 3:22 … ἐν ἁπλότητι καρδίας, φοβούμενοι τὸν κύριον (variant θεόν: 𝔓⁴⁶ א² D² 𝔪 — check the NA28 apparatus).
- Stated: φοβ- with (τὸν) κύριον is an LXX formula in **about 70 Swete verses**; **Col 3:22 is the only NT verse with the verb φοβέομαι + τὸν κύριον as object**; in context "the Lord" is Christ (3:24 τῷ κυρίῳ Χριστῷ), so the OT fear of YHWH is directed to Christ. 2 Cor 5:10–11 also joins receiving back with "the fear of the Lord" (τὸν φόβον τοῦ κυρίου). Suggested **moderate–high**.
- Test: reproduce the Swete count (define the pattern clearly: φοβ- verb or noun with κύριος/κυρίου as object/genitive in one verse; report both the verb-only and verb-or-noun counts); the NT count by lemma (φοβέομαι with κύριος object; φόβος with κυρίου: Acts 9:31; 2 Cor 5:11; Eph 5:21 φόβῳ Χριστοῦ); the variant's weight (if θεόν were original the claim lapses); is the referent Christ in 3:22 (3:23–24) or ambiguous? Verdict.
