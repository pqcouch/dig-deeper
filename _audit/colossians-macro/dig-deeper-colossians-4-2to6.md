## Preaching Pitfalls

### Pitfall: "Pray for open doors" as prayer for my plans

- **What it looks like:** "Pray that God will open doors for my job, my move, my project."
- **Why it's wrong:** the door is "for the word", to speak "the mystery of Christ", requested by a man in chains.
- **The corrective:** keep the door's purpose — the word's free course — and the cost that came with it.

### Pitfall: "Redeem the time" as time management

- **What it looks like:** a sermon on productivity and wasted hours.
- **Why it's wrong:** καιρός is opportunity, the setting is "towards outsiders", and the next verse is about answering people.
- **The corrective:** preach alert opportunism for the gospel, in ordinary relationships.

### Pitfall: Salt as licence for sharpness

- **What it looks like:** "Salty" speech — biting, combative, clever at others' expense.
- **Why it's wrong:** the speech is "always with grace" first; the aim is to answer "each one", not to win.
- **The corrective:** grace governs, salt flavours.

### Pitfall: Evangelism as a technique

- **What it looks like:** turning 4:5–6 into a scripted method.
- **Why it's wrong:** the verbs are "walk" and "answer" — a life that prompts questions and a word fitted to each person.
- **The corrective:** preach a watchful, prayerful, gracious life and readiness to answer, with prayer for the preachers as its partner.

### Pitfall: Skipping 4:2 to reach the "mission" verses

- **What it looks like:** treating prayer as preface.
- **Why it's wrong:** the passage's first and main command is prayer; the mission depends on God opening doors.
- **The corrective:** give 4:2–4 at least equal weight.
